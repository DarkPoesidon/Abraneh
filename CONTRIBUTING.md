# Contributing

Thank you for helping. Before you open a pull request, please read this.

## Contributor agreement

By submitting a contribution (code, text, images or anything else) to this repository, you agree that:

1. You wrote it yourself, or have the right to submit it.
2. You grant DarkPoesidon a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, change and distribute your contribution under any license, including the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0, a later license and separate commercial licenses.
3. Your contribution may be published with your name in the project's history.

If you can't agree, please open an issue describing the change instead of sending code.

## How to contribute

- Open an issue first for anything larger than a small fix.
- Keep each pull request to one change, with tests where they apply. See [docs/DEVELOPMENT.fa.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.fa.md).
- Don't remove copyright lines, the Required Notice or credits. See [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md).
- Report security problems privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
